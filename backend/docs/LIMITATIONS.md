# Limitations

This is a scaffold, not an operational grid analysis. The planned DC approximation omits voltage magnitude, reactive power, protection dynamics, transient stability, market actions, operator intervention, and detailed relay behavior. Island balancing is a modeling choice and cannot stand in for a validated utility procedure. Ratings, thresholds, and severity weights require case-specific authority.

No utility topology or confidential operational data is bundled. The toy fixture, if added later, is for software checks only. Quantum modules cannot establish practical speedup without complete oracle costs, data loading, verification, hardware noise, and a fair classical baseline. Results are not forecasts, safety advice, or evidence of blackout likelihood.
